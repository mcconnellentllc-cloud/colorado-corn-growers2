SDRP Sign-Up Closes September 30 — Last Call for 2023–2024 Disaster Losses

USDA extended the SDRP application deadline a second time, to September 30, 2026, for both Stage 1 and Stage 2. The payment factor holds at 70%. FSA also added flexibility on how 2023–2024 quality losses can be documented — and warned that producers still waiting on FSA to generate a Stage 1 quality loss application must file by the deadline anyway.

September 18, 2026

CCGA Presses for a Permanent Disaster Program in the Farm Bill

CCGA was at the table when Rep. Lauren Boebert convened an agriculture round table at the Cow Palace in Lamar. President Jeff Self and Past President Dave Cure made the case for writing a permanent disaster program into the farm bill — a standing program that reaches producers who actually take a loss, without propping up operations that sustain none. A permanent program would also give producers something they can plan against, instead of waiting on an ad hoc bill after every disaster.

August 28, 2026

Eminent Domain on the Eastern Plains: The Case For and Against

A Cheyenne County judge blocked Xcel from condemning farmland for a power line serving its own wind farms, and dismissed the case. Xcel has appealed to the Colorado Supreme Court. The outcome sets the baseline for what every eastern Colorado landowner can ask for when energy infrastructure crosses their ground. Both sides laid out for member discussion — CCGA has taken no position.
