Shared logins, encrypted. Locked with the board passphrase.

How this is protected. Entries are encrypted in your browser with your passphrase — only the scrambled form is ever stored, here and in the file you export. The passphrase is never saved and never leaves this page. Choose a strong one, keep it off the site and out of email, and make it different from the portal password.
