DRAFT — NOT IN FORCE. This document was assembled to show the board what the terms need to cover and to give a Colorado attorney something concrete to mark up. It has not been reviewed by counsel. Do not enroll anyone under it and do not present it to a producer as binding.

Two things in particular need a lawyer rather than a draftsman: the limitation of liability in section 6 and the indemnity in section 7. Waivers covering information and advice are read narrowly by Colorado courts, and an indemnity that reaches too far can be struck in whole rather than trimmed.

Farm Program Guides & Updates — Program Terms

Draft version 0.1 · Prepared September 25, 2026 · Unexecuted

1. What This Program Is

The Colorado Corn Growers Association (“CCGA”) publishes written guides, deadline alerts and updates concerning federal and state agricultural programs (together, the “Materials”). Enrolling gives you the Materials for the enrollment term. It gives you nothing else.

2. What This Program Is Not

  • CCGA does not administer any USDA, FSA, RMA or state program, and has no authority to approve, deny, expedite, appeal or influence any determination affecting you.
  • The Materials are general information. They are not legal advice, tax advice, accounting advice, financial advice, or insurance advice, and they do not create a professional or advisory relationship of any kind.
  • Enrollment does not make you a client. CCGA is not acting as your agent, representative or fiduciary.
  • Nothing in the Materials is a determination of your eligibility, your payment, or your compliance with any program requirement.

3. Your Decisions Are Yours

You are responsible for your own operation. Before acting on anything in the Materials you should confirm it with your FSA county office, RMA, your crop insurance agent, your accountant or your attorney, as appropriate. Your FSA county office is the only source that can tell you your actual eligibility and your actual payment.

4. Accuracy and Corrections

CCGA prepares the Materials in good faith from public sources. Program rules, deadlines, payment rates and eligibility change, sometimes mid-season and sometimes without notice. CCGA does not warrant that the Materials are complete, current or free of error.

When CCGA learns that something it published was wrong, CCGA will correct it in writing to enrolled producers, identifying what was wrong and what is correct.

5. No Guarantee of Outcome

CCGA makes no representation that enrolling will result in any payment, any benefit, or any particular outcome. No employee, director or volunteer of CCGA is authorized to make such a representation, and any that is made is void.

6. Limitation of Liability

[FOR COUNSEL: enforceability, permitted scope under Colorado law, and whether a cap at fees paid is defensible for an information product.]

To the fullest extent permitted by Colorado law, CCGA’s total liability to you arising out of or relating to this program is limited to the fees you paid for the current enrollment term. CCGA is not liable for indirect, incidental, consequential or punitive damages, including lost payments, lost benefits, missed deadlines, or lost profits, even if advised of the possibility.

Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including liability for fraud or willful misconduct.

7. Release and Indemnification

[FOR COUNSEL: scope, whether a release of unknown claims is valid here, and whether requiring indemnification from a producer is proportionate for a $500 information product or is likely to be struck.]

You release CCGA and its directors, officers, employees and volunteers from claims arising out of your use of the Materials or your own program decisions, and you agree to indemnify and hold them harmless against third-party claims arising out of your use of the Materials, except to the extent caused by CCGA’s fraud or willful misconduct.

8. Fees, Term and Refunds

Enrollment is $500 per operation for a twelve-month term, beginning when CCGA confirms enrollment. [FOR BOARD: set the refund policy. Recommend a stated window — full refund within 30 days, prorated or none thereafter. A program with no refund policy at all invites the dispute it is trying to avoid.]

9. Your Own Compliance

You are responsible for the accuracy of anything you file with any agency. Submitting false information to USDA carries penalties that fall on you and not on CCGA.

10. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.

11. Changes

CCGA may revise these terms for future enrollment terms. The version in force for you is the version you accepted, identified by version number and date, and CCGA retains a copy of it.

Acceptance

Enrollment requires affirmative acceptance of these terms. CCGA records, for each enrollment: the producer and operation, the version number and date of the terms accepted, the timestamp of acceptance, and the exact text in force at that moment. A copy is emailed to the producer at acceptance.
