Colorado Corn Growers Association

Put your name beside the work that keeps Colorado farming

We are not the checkoff board. We are volunteer corn producers who get the bills through Denver and Washington — funded entirely by the businesses and growers who decide this work is worth paying for.

A grower who gets a disaster payment through, or keeps a farm solvent through a bad year, is a grower who is still your customer next spring. This association is the reason a number of them are — and sponsorship puts your name where those growers see it, all year.

What this association has delivered

  • Over $1.2 billion secured for Colorado agriculture since 2018
  • E15 and the Renewable Fuel Standard defended — ethanol demand is corn demand
  • T-yields raised for corn across Colorado, lifting safety-net payments
  • $8.6 million for the Gold Hill Pipeline at Greeley
