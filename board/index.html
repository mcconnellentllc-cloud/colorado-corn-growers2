About this portal

Access is limited to the association’s current board roster. There is no self-registration, and addresses that are not on the roster receive nothing. If you are a board member and cannot get in, contact the association office rather than trying a different address.

Every sign-in and every ballot cast is recorded with a timestamp for the association’s records.
